# MAHAGAURI — The Stillness That Moved a River
### My version: director's treatment + shoot-ready production pack (60s master)

**Logline:** A Himalayan princess sits so still for love that the seasons, the mountains and a heavenly river change course around her — and when grace finally descends, it stops in mid-air… and waits for *her* to say yes.

**Thesis vs. your cut:** Your film = heaven acts on her (spectacle peaks, maximal camera, river collapses *on* her, she mounts the bull). My film = her stillness acts on heaven. Three reversals:

1. **The camera learns tapas** — restless → completely locked-off until 30s; only the world moves. After the bath, the camera flows *with* her for the first time. Stillness → flow is felt in the viewer's body.
2. **The river asks permission** — Peak 2 is not impact, it's a drop that halts above her closed fist until she stands and opens her palms. Consent = agency = the emotional peak.
3. **Night is not dirt** — the dark sheath lifts as a star-filled veil (Kaushiki) and ascends, honoured. Faithful to the Kaushiki telling, comment-proof, and discussable.

**Ending:** the bull *kneels* (chooses her); she turns her abhaya (blessing) hand to camera. Hand match-cuts to Shot 1's bead-hand = the loop.

**Peak map (60s):** 12 / 27 / 42 / 56 — same spacing logic as yours (3–5s releases; 20–30s releases only belong in the 90s/3-min expansions below). But Peak 2 is *silence*: contrast, not volume.

**Re-hook ladder:** 0s Why is everything moving except her? · 4s What has she given up? (Aparna leaf) · 9s Who is listening? (damaru heartbeat, no source) · 14s Why is the mountain answering? · 19s Why did the river stop? · 24s Will she open her hands? · 31s What is leaving her body? (Kaushiki veil) · 38s Is that still her? · 46s Why is the bull kneeling? · 57s Who is she blessing? (us → loop)
